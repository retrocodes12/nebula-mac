**Downloads.** Keep a film or an episode on your iPhone or Mac and watch it with no connection. A film's page has Download, an episode has it in its menu, and a season has "Download Season N" above its episodes: pick a source once and the other episodes follow with the same release. My List shows your downloads with Pause, Resume, Retry and Remove; a downloaded title plays its own copy. On the iPhone a download keeps going with the app closed and carries on from where it stopped after a pause or a dropped connection. Only finished files are kept; live, protected and web-page streams, and streams that come in pieces, can't be downloaded.

**Brightness and volume swipes** on the iPhone's player: up or down on the left half sets the brightness (given back when you leave the player), on the right half the volume.

**Autoplay's add-on chips** wrap onto a second line on a narrow screen instead of squeezing.
